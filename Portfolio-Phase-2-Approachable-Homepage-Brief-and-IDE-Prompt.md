## 1. Objective

Make the homepage immediately understandable to non-technical business owners, SME operators, community administrators, and organization leaders without removing its technical depth or premium visual identity.

The visitor should understand within 10–15 seconds:

1. Who Ahlul Firdaus is.
2. What kinds of problems he helps solve.
3. What he can design or build.
4. What work already exists.
5. How to start a conversation.

Phase 2 is an information-hierarchy and plain-language improvement. It is not a full redesign.

Guiding principle:

> Clarity first. Evidence second. Technical depth when the visitor wants it.

## 6. Section: Problems I Can Help Simplify

### Eyebrow

**START WITH THE PROBLEM**

### Heading

**Does Any of This Feel Familiar?**

### Intro

You do not need to arrive with a technical specification. A useful project can begin with a recurring problem, an existing spreadsheet, or a process that has become difficult to manage.

### Problem list

- Information is scattered across WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and individual files.
- Reports are repetitive or difficult to consolidate.
- Teams cannot easily see what is complete, delayed, or still waiting.
- Customers or members struggle to find the information or service they need.
- Administrative work depends too heavily on one person's memory.
- The same data is entered in several places.
- An existing website or application has become confusing to use.
- You have an idea but need help turning it into a realistic first version.

### Closing line

**Show me how the work happens today. We can identify a practical improvement from there.**

Do not claim that every problem requires custom software. A review, simpler website, reporting template, or workflow adjustment may be the appropriate result.

## 9. Section: How I Work

Keep the methodology but translate it into plain language.

### Heading

**From Problem to Practical First Version**

### Step 1 — Understand

Clarify the current problem, users, workflow, constraints, and desired outcome.

### Step 2 — Structure

Organize the information, user journey, responsibilities, and core product scope.

### Step 3 — Design

Create a clear interface around the real workflow rather than decorative screens.

### Step 4 — Build

Develop the agreed first version using an appropriate modern web stack and AI-assisted workflow.

### Step 5 — Validate

Check functionality, responsiveness, accessibility, content, and realistic edge cases.

### Step 6 — Improve

Launch or hand over the current version, document limitations, and identify the next priorities.

Avoid guarantees such as eliminating project risk or ensuring compliance.

## 16. Responsive and Accessibility Requirements

- Maintain a logical heading hierarchy.
- Keep card text readable without very small type.
- Ensure CTA buttons wrap or stack cleanly on mobile.
- Avoid horizontally scrolling architecture content unless it has clear controls and an accessible alternative.
- Ensure all tabs, accordions, filters, cards, forms, and simulations are keyboard operable.
- Preserve visible focus states.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Maintain sufficient contrast for muted copy.
- Provide descriptive form labels and error messages.
- Do not rely on color alone for project status or completion state.
- Prevent layout shift from images and dynamic content.
- Maintain tap targets appropriate for touch screens.

- The hero is understandable without technical knowledge.
- Only two dominant hero CTAs are present.
- What I Help You Build appears near the top.
- Problems I Can Help Simplify uses concrete, recognizable problems.
- Selected Work remains visible before dense technical content.
- Project cards use standardized status and evidence language.
- Services use Review & Clarify, Design & Build, and Improve & Support.
- The process is explained in plain language.
- Architecture and artifacts remain available to technical reviewers.
- Biography tells the authentic operations-to-digital story.
- No generated/fake portrait is introduced.
- Unverified testimonials are not restored.
- The Indonesian client section is present without implementing unnecessary full localization.
- Contact copy does not require a technical specification.
- CTA wording is consistent.
- Resume/CV remains prominent and downloadable.
- Mobile layout is readable and stable.
- Keyboard, focus, contrast, forms, and reduced-motion behavior remain accessible.
- Phase 1 credibility corrections remain intact.
- Existing validation and production build pass, or pre-existing failures are documented.
- No unrelated redesign or project-feature development occurs.
